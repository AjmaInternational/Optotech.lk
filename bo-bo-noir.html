Bo Bo Noir

Handcrafted in Portugal with a commitment to sustainable elegance and dark-toned aesthetics.

Philosophy

The Beauty of Shadow.

Bo Bo Noir explores the intersection of traditional craftsmanship and avant-garde design. Every frame is a testament to the artisan's touch.

"A frame should not hide the face, but define the soul behind it."

Combining Portuguese cork elements with Italian acetates, Bo Bo Noir creates a sensory experience that is as much about feel as it is about vision. Available exclusively at Optotech for the Negombo region.
